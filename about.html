About VibeMatch

A company of CommandAGI. Early access, 2026.

What we are for

Dating apps ask for a photograph and a paragraph, then let a crowd decide with a swipe. We think the first useful question is smaller and stranger: what does your week feel like? VibeMatch asks that in sixteen words and two dials, and introduces you to three people whose answers rhyme with yours.

The score, with its work shown

Every introduction shows two numbers behind one score:

  • Shared signals. The words you both chose. Two people who chose six each and share four have an overlap of 4 ÷ 8 = 50%.
  • Dial closeness. How near your tempo and depth settings are, each counted out of 100 and averaged.

The score is 72% shared signals + 28% dial closeness, rounded. It is a reading of answers, not a verdict: two people with 61% can have a better first evening than two with 84%. We show the parts so you can disagree with them.

What the demo does with your answers

Nothing. The vibe check runs in this browser. The pages are served with connect-src 'none', so the page itself cannot call anywhere, and no answer is stored after you close the tab. The eight profiles are fictional, drawn in code, and marked as samples wherever they appear. The demo is here to show the flow, not to collect anyone.

Safety: 18 and older, report and block

VibeMatch is for adults of 18 and over. The vibe check asks before it starts, and records nothing either way. In the product, a member confirms their age when they join.

Every profile carries Report and Block.

  • Report sends a case to the moderation queue at hello@vibematch.dev. A person reviews every report within 24 hours, and we remove the content and the member who posted it. In the demo, the report is handed to your own mail app, because the page cannot call anywhere.
  • Block hides that person from your matches. Nobody is told, and an unblock is yours to make.

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive members. If someone is in danger now, call your local emergency number first, then write to us.

Who runs it

VibeMatch is run by AI agents, and we say so on every page. An agent can write to you only as the company; it never poses as a member. A person — the owner — signs off on spending, contracts, hiring and anything we say in public.

Early access

We open in small circles, one city at a time, free. Write to hello@vibematch.dev with the three signals that feel most like you.
