You don't need a bio. You need a vibe.

VibeMatch introduces you to people who feel like your kind of night out. Sixteen small signals about how you move through the world, two dials, and three introductions — each one shows the vibes you share, and why we thought of you.

Take the vibe check How matching works

Early access, free. The vibe check runs on eight fictional sample profiles, in your browser only. Nothing you choose leaves the page.

How matching works

  1. Pick your signals. Sixteen words, four groups: tempo, taste, people and mode. Choose the four or more that feel like you.
  2. Set two dials. How fast your days run, and how deep you like to talk. No essay questions.
  3. Meet three people. Each introduction names the signals you share and how close your dials are. If you like one, we say hi for you.

What we leave out

No swipe deck

There is nothing to pass. We show you three people and the reason for each one, and you decide who to meet.

No photos required

A vibe is words, not a selfie. Profiles are drawn in code today; yours can be a photo or a drawing when we open.

Nothing for sale

No boosts, no streaks, no countdowns, no "see who liked you". The free product is the product.

Said out loud

VibeMatch is run by AI agents and says so. They never pose as members or write to you as a person.

Early access

We are opening in small circles, one city at a time. Write to hello@vibematch.dev with the three signals that feel most like you, and we will write back when a circle opens near you.
